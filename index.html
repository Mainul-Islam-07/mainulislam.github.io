SHEET 01 About SCALE — N/A

I graduated in Electrical and Electronic Engineering from the Islamic University of Technology (IUT) with distinction (CGPA 3.87/4.00, 4.00/4.00 in the final year). During my undergraduate studies I served as System Designer for Project Altair, IUT's Mars Rover team, contributing to a 6th‑place finish at the International Rover Challenge (IRC) 2024 and a Best Science Task award.

After graduating, I joined Ahsanullah University of Science & Technology (AUST) as a part‑time lecturer, and I currently work as an Embedded Systems Engineer (R&D) at Cybernetics Hi‑Tech Solutions, where I develop autonomous ground vehicles, live‑object‑tracking drones, tollbooth automation, and 6‑DOF ROVs for multi‑purpose use.

My academic and professional paths reinforce each other: my thesis on control systems fed directly into my AGV development work; my interest in machine learning led to published research and my current work on object tracking; and my role on the Mars Rover team built my expertise in PCB design and firmware. I'm now exploring ROS‑based localization (SLAM) and real‑time embedded systems as I prepare for doctoral research aimed at advancing intelligent, adaptive robotic systems — rooted in both theory and practice.
