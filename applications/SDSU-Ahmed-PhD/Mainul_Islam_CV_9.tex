\section*{Professional Experience}

\cvitem{Research and Development Engineer / Embedded Systems Engineer --- Cybernetics Hi-Tech Solutions Pvt. Ltd. \normalfont\small(\href{https://www.youtube.com/@CyberneticsHi-Tech}{link})}{Oct 2024 -- Present}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Autonomous Ground Vehicle (AGV):} Co-developed a from-scratch Extended Kalman Filter fusing dual-camera and wheel-odometry data with AprilTag-based visual pose estimation (roll/pitch/yaw); implemented LiDAR-based obstacle avoidance, Modbus encoder feedback, and coordinate-frame transformations linking robot, camera, and tag frames.     \item \textbf{Man-Portable Teleoperated Robot:} Designed a fault-tolerant CAN-bus layer controlling 11 motors with heartbeat, fault, and battery-based autonomous response, and a ROS2 middleware layer above it that bridged two CAN networks, unified inverse-kinematics control and discrete command mapping. Built a fiber-optic/wireless failover communication link for SBUS control data. Developed a custom inverse-kinematics solver for a 4-DOF subsystem of a 7-DOF arm (turret, shoulder, wrist, telescopic joint); engineered a low-latency (\textless280 ms) 5-camera RTSP application with real-time 3D URDF/STL visualization, touchscreen gimbal control, dual-channel audio, and full motor/register diagnostics.
    \item \textbf{Toll-Booth Automation:} Developed event-driven barrier control (vehicle detection, payment, theft detection) with loop-coil vehicle sensing and license-plate recognition; designed and manufactured a compact custom PCB with tamper protection.
