Man Transportable EOD ROV

An Explosive Ordnance Disposal (EOD) Remotely Operated Vehicle (ROV) Designed to Be Carried and Deployed by a Soldier.
Currently, we are developing a man-transportable EOD ROV based on our experience of previously developed medium platform EOD ROV, Jontro Soinik v1.0. Initially, we analyzed the limitations of previous robot and new challenges to develop a small platform robot.
