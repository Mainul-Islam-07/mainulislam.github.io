Currently, we are developing a man-transportable EOD ROV, building on our experience with the previously developed medium-platform EOD ROV, Jontro Soinik v1.0. We began by analyzing the limitations of the earlier robot and identifying new challenges associated with designing a smaller platform. In the next phase, our focus was on constructing a lightweight structure that maximizes strength, maintains a low center of gravity, and introduces a new degree of freedom in the arm through a screw-based telescopic beam. To further reduce weight and enhance mechanical performance, we are integrating carbon fiber components into the robot. These parts are fabricated in-house using resin infusion, compression molding, and hand layup techniques within our composite fabrication laboratory.

The Man-Transportable EOD ROV is a compact bomb disposal robot built for high-stakes counter-IED operations. It navigates complex terrain with ease, using a dual-mode drive system and adaptive flipper mechanisms. The detachable manipulator arm offers precise control and tool-mounting flexibility, making it ideal for handling hazardous materials. Operators benefit from real-time situational awareness through a multi-camera setup, including a PTZ unit and wrist-mounted views. The robot's control unit features a daylight-readable display and supports both wired and wireless communication for uninterrupted mission execution. Its rugged build and shock-absorbing design ensure durability in harsh environments. Integrated overload protection and intelligent motion control enhance operational safety and reliability. Designed for tactical deployment, the Man-Transportable EOD ROV combines mobility, modularity, and mission-ready resilience in a compact form.
