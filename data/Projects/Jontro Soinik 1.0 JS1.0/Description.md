Jontro Soinik 1.0 (JS1.0) is Bangladesh's third generation and most advanced indigenously developed Explosive Ordnance Disposal (EOD) Remotely Operated Vehicle (ROV) with environmental adaptability and disrupter firing capacity, which is first of its kind, engineered for rapid deployment and rugged performance in high-risk environments. Successfully tested by the Bangladesh Army, including live disrupter firing and IP55 water protection trials, it proves its readiness for real-world missions. Equipped with advanced electronics like GPS, thermal imaging, IR night vision, 4x optical zoom, and a 2MP camera system, it supports precision surveillance and remote neutralization of threats. With robust mechanical capabilities—stair climbing, slope handling (up to 40°), 360° claw rotation, 40kg grip force, and a lifting capacity of up to 20kg—the ROV is built for maneuverability and strength. Its 16Ah battery enables extended operations with active/passive overload protection, self-diagnostics, and fast recharging. Designed for battlefield reliability, Jontro Soinik 1.0 embodies innovation, adaptability, and national pride in defense technology. Jontro Soinik 1.0 is set for deployment in 2025 under the UN Peacekeeping Mission in the Republic of the Congo.
