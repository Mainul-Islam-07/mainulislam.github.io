Novel two-stage adaptive training (YOLOv8m)

Stage 1 (35 ep): aggressive feature learning · progressive unfreezing 12→8→4 · spatial aug (mosaic, mix-up, copy-paste). Stage 2 (45/55 ep, adaptive on Stage-1 mAP): photometric aug · reduced spatial · cosine-annealed LR · dynamic convergence checking.

Engineering highlights
  • Phase-aware training regimen — augmentation & layer-freezing adapt to the training phase; Stage 2 auto-selects 45 vs 55 epochs on convergence.
  • OOD validation by design — curated a 276-image low-light CCTV set to prove generalization, not just in-domain accuracy.
  • Domain-correct decoding — disabled n-gram repetition blocking so valid repeated digits (১১, ১১-১১) aren't suppressed.
  • Diagnosed the mBART anomaly — lowest WER yet worst CER: word-level right, intra-word characters wrong.
