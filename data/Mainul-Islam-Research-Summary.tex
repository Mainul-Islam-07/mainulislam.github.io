\vspace{2pt}

My background sits at the intersection of embedded control systems, robotics, and applied
machine learning. Across five research projects and two years of hands-on robotics
engineering, I have moved from designing the low-level control and communication backbone of
multi-node robotic systems to validating control algorithms in simulation and applying deep
learning to real-world perception problems. I am seeking a Ph.D.\ position to formalize this
experience into rigorous research on AI-enabled digital twins and control co-design for
autonomous systems.

\section*{Control Systems, Robotics \& Simulation}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{MonoSIM} (published, IEEE): Co-developed an open-source Software-in-the-Loop
    framework for Ackermann-steered vehicles, built to validate control algorithms before
    hardware deployment. Implemented and compared Model Predictive Control (MPC) and PID
    controllers against a monocular-vision lane-detection pipeline --- direct experience with
    the simulate-design-validate loop central to control co-design and digital twins.

    \item \textbf{Multi-node Real-Time Control} (published, IEEE ICCIT): Designed and timed a
    master-slave RS485/UART communication architecture across 11+ STM32 microcontrollers
    coordinating a 6-DOF robotic arm and drive system, using DMA and CRC for reliable,
    low-latency feedback --- foundational experience in the real-time data pipelines that
    AI-enabled digital twins depend on.

    \item \textbf{Teleoperated Robotic Manipulator} (``Manportable''): Built the system
    architecture for an 11-motor, dual-CAN-bus robot with a custom inverse-kinematics solver
    for a 7-DOF arm, ROS2-based integration with full sensor feedback, and fault/failsafe
    logic --- practical exposure to the design-control interaction that motivates control
    co-design.

    \item \textbf{Autonomous Ground Vehicle}: Contributed an Extended Kalman Filter built from
    scratch to fuse dual-camera AprilTag detections with wheel odometry, and helped build the
    ROS2 stack for localization, path planning, and LIDAR-based obstacle avoidance --- core
    state-estimation skills for model-based and learning-based control.
\end{itemize}

\section*{Machine Learning \& Deep Learning}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Bangla License Plate Recognition} (published, ICARA): Designed a two-stage
    adaptive YOLOv8 training strategy with phase-aware augmentation for detection, paired with
    a ViT + BanglaBERT OCR pipeline, validated on real low-light CCTV data --- hands-on deep
    learning model design and rigorous benchmarking.

    \item \textbf{Solar Panel Fault Detection} (published, Solar Energy): Contributed to a
    hybrid RefineU-Net segmentation and MobileNetV3 classification framework for multi-class
    fault detection, incorporating explainable AI (LIME) for interpretability --- experience
    with efficient architectures and trustworthy-model considerations relevant to AI-driven
    decision-making.

    \item \textbf{Long-Term Energy Demand Forecasting} (published, IEEE): Benchmarked Random
    Forest, KNN, XGBoost, and LightGBM on a million-sample utility dataset for long-term
    demand prediction --- direct experience with data-driven modeling and comparative
    optimization of learning algorithms.
\end{itemize}

\section*{Technical Skills}
Python (PyTorch, scikit-learn), C/C++ (embedded, STM32), ROS2, real-time embedded control
(UART, RS485, CAN bus, Modbus, DMA), classical estimation and control (EKF, PID, MPC), PCB
design, Git, and end-to-end deployment of vision and forecasting models from data collection
through publication.

\section*{Research Interests}
My core research interests span control systems, machine learning, computer vision, embedded
systems \& IoT, autonomous systems, and robotics.

My experience validating controllers in simulation before hardware deployment (MonoSIM) and
building the real-time sensing/communication backbone for multi-agent robots naturally
extends toward AI-enabled digital twins: systems that continuously learn from live data to
inform design and control decisions. I am especially drawn to control co-design that couples
reinforcement learning with physical design optimization, and to multi-fidelity,
simulation-grounded approaches for dynamic autonomous and robotic systems, and I am eager to
bring my combined control, embedded-systems, and deep-learning background to that work.
