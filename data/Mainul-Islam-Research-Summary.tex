This summary spans seven publications (five published, two under review) and hands-on projects across

\section*{Research Interests}
Building on my experience across control, machine learning, and embedded robotics, I am interested in
pursuing future research on:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Learning-based and adaptive control (including reinforcement learning) for autonomous and
    multi-agent robotic systems.
    \item Robust perception, sensor fusion, and state estimation for reliable autonomy in unstructured,
    real-world environments.
    \item AI-enabled digital twins and control co-design that couple simulation, learning, and
    physical-system design.
    \item Efficient, interpretable, and trustworthy machine learning for edge and embedded deployment
    in safety-critical applications.
    \item Real-time, fault-tolerant embedded and cyber-physical systems for field robotics and IoT.
\end{itemize}

\section*{Control Systems, Autonomous Systems \& Robotics}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{MonoSIM} \pub{IEEE ICARA}: Open-source Software-in-the-Loop framework for
    Ackermann-steered autonomous vehicles; implemented and benchmarked Model Predictive Control (MPC)
    and PID against a monocular-vision lane-detection pipeline --- the simulate--design--validate loop
    central to control co-design and digital twins.
    \item \textbf{Speed Synchronization of a Dual-BLDC Tracked Vehicle} \rev: GA-tuned multi-rate
    cross-coupled controller for precise speed synchronization of a differential-drive tracked platform.
    \item \textbf{Autonomous Ground Vehicle --- CYBERFLEET} (project): ROS2 autonomy stack for a
    3-robot warehouse fleet --- a from-scratch 5-state EKF fusing AprilTag vision with wheel odometry,
    space-time A* planning, trajectory tracking, and LiDAR obstacle avoidance.
    \item \textbf{Man-Transportable EOD ROV (JS2.0)} (project): System architecture for an 11-motor,
    dual-CAN robot --- closed-form 7-DOF inverse kinematics (verified with MoveIt 2), redundant
    wired/wireless control, and layered fail-safe logic.
    \item \textbf{EOD ROVs --- Jontro Soinik 1.0 \& Smart Soinik 1.0} (delivered): Real-time embedded
    firmware and addressable-UART modular control for field-deployed robots operating under the
    Bangladesh Army and a UN Peacekeeping Mission.
    \item \textbf{Autonomous Target-Tracking Drone} (project): Onboard-vision detection and tracking
    (YOLO on Raspberry Pi 5; DeepSORT/KCF) integrated with the flight controller.
    \item \textbf{Surveillance Drone (SD15-N)} (project) \& \textbf{Fixed-Wing VTOL UAV} (project):
    Long-endurance-UAV flight-time optimization (drag/thrust/battery), and a prototype study of
    aerodynamic stability, control allocation, and VTOL-to-fixed-wing transition.
\end{itemize}

\section*{Machine Learning, Computer Vision \& Deep Learning}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Bangla License Plate Recognition} \pub{IEEE ICAD}: Two-stage adaptive YOLOv8
    detector paired with a ViT + BanglaBERT vision-language OCR pipeline, validated on real low-light
    CCTV data.
    \item \textbf{Solar Panel Fault Detection} \pub{Solar Energy, IF 6.6}: Hybrid RefineU-Net
    segmentation and MobileNetV3 classification for multi-class PV fault detection, with LIME-based
    explainable AI for interpretability.
    \item \textbf{Long-Term Energy Demand Forecasting} \pub{IEEE ICECIE}: Benchmarked Random Forest,
    KNN, XGBoost, and LightGBM on a million-sample utility dataset for long-horizon demand prediction.
\end{itemize}

\section*{Embedded Systems, Power Electronics \& Hardware}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Multi-node Real-Time Control} \pub{IEEE ICCIT}: Master--slave RS485/UART
    architecture across 11+ STM32 microcontrollers with DMA and CRC for reliable, low-latency
    distributed control.
    \item \textbf{Topological Survey of Zeta Power Converters} \rev: Comparative study of Zeta
    converter topologies, from classical designs to emerging architectures.
    \item \textbf{Toll-Booth Automation} (project): Industrial multi-lane STM32 controller ---
    table-driven FSM firmware, DMA-UART and WIZnet W5500 TCP networking, and three custom PCBs with
    galvanic isolation.
    \item \textbf{Offline Hardware CryptoWallet} (project): Air-gapped embedded wallet --- system
    state machine, touchscreen UI, BIP-39 key generation, and QR-based offline transaction signing.
    \item \textbf{Undergraduate \& team hardware}: 8-bit SAP-1 computer; LiPo Battery Management
    System; pulse-width measurement/generation on the AT89S52; and IUT Mars Rover (Project Altair,
    Team Avijatrik) --- PCB design, hardware integration, and debugging for international rover challenges.
\end{itemize}
